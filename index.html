About AALA Pharma

Committed to Health, Driven by Pharmaceutical Excellence

Headquartered at Quila, Aonla, Bareilly (UP), AALA Pharma is built on a mission to democratize essential healthcare. We specialize in producing premium-grade generic medications and branded formulations that meet the strictest international pharmacopeia standards (IP, BP, USP).

Our manufacturing plants utilize advanced cleanroom technologies, computerized batch processing, and comprehensive quality assurance systems to serve clinics, hospitals, distributors, and pharmacy chains nationwide.

  • WHO-GMP certified oral solid dosage, liquid syrup, and sterile injectable lines
  • Dedicated microbiological and physicochemical testing laboratories
  • Robust supply chain offering transparent PCD Franchise & Contract Manufacturing
