World-Class Manufacturing & Quality Control

Upholding unyielding standards of purity, safety, and regulatory compliance through computerized cleanroom facilities and sophisticated analytical instrumentation.

Modern Infrastructure

State-of-the-Art Formulation Plant in Bareilly

Located in Quila, Aonla, Bareilly, AALA Pharma’s manufacturing campus is engineered to meet WHO-GMP and schedule-M guidelines. The plant incorporates dedicated HVAC air handling units (AHUs) with HEPA filtration providing ISO Class 7 and Class 8 controlled environments.

With unidirectional men-material movement, separate sampling and dispensing booths with dynamic laminar air flows, and zero cross-contamination design, our facility guarantees complete batch-to-batch consistency.

  • High-speed rotary tablet compression machines with automated weight control
  • Automatic capsule filling units with electronic pin-hole inspection
  • High-precision sterile liquid filling lines with online CIP/SIP systems
Dual-Tier Assurance

Quality Assurance & Analytical Control

Our quality philosophy: Quality is not tested into products; it is engineered and built into every stage of production.

Quality Assurance (QA)

Autonomous QA division managing validation protocols, change controls, deviation management, vendor audits, Annual Product Quality Reviews (APQR), and batch record certifications prior to commercial release.

Quality Control (QC)

Equipped with high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), UV-Vis spectrophotometers, gas chromatographs (GC), Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), and automatic digital dissolution testers.

Microbiological Testing

Sterility testing suites, bacterial endotoxin testing (LAL test), environmental particulate monitoring, bioburden quantification, and water-for-injection (WFI) loop microbial monitoring.

Protocol

The Five-Stage Quality Lifecycle

A comprehensive lifecycle ensuring total safety from raw molecule to finished formulation.

Raw Material Qualification

100% identity testing, assay verification, and impurity profiling of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and excipients.

In-Process Controls (IPQC)

Continuous monitoring of blend uniformity, hardness, friability, disintegration time, fill volume, and viscosity during manufacturing.

Finished Product Assays

Release testing against pharmacopeial specifications (IP/BP/USP) ensuring exact potency, dissolution rate, and content uniformity.

Stability Studies (ICH Zones)

Continuous real-time and accelerated stability chambers (Zone IVb: 30°C/75% RH & 40°C/75% RH) validating shelf-life integrity.

Patient First

Pharmacovigilance & Drug Safety

At AALA Pharma, patient safety is our highest priority. We maintain a dedicated Pharmacovigilance cell that actively monitors the safety profile of our marketed formulations in accordance with the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI).

Healthcare professionals, distributors, and patients can report any adverse event, suspected side effect, or packaging deviation directly to our dedicated pharmacovigilance officer.
