The AALA Pharma Story

Rooted in Purpose, Built on Pharmaceutical Integrity

Founded with the conviction that no patient should be denied access to quality medicines due to cost or geographic barrier, AALA Pharma has evolved into a powerhouse of pharmaceutical manufacturing located in Quila, Aonla, Bareilly (UP).

What began as a focused formulations facility has expanded into a multi-dosage manufacturing center with advanced capabilities spanning tablets, hard gelatin capsules, liquid orals, sterile dry powder injectables, and topical semi-solids.

By investing in automated equipment, strict quality assurance protocols, and a dedicated team of pharmacists and chemists, AALA Pharma partners with thousands of healthcare practitioners, hospitals, and institutional buyers to provide reliable treatments everyday.

Milestones

A Journey of Steady Advancement

Key chapters that define the growth of AALA Pharma over the decades.

Inception & Foundation

Established manufacturing footprint in Aonla, Bareilly with dedicated oral solid dosage processing units and central quality laboratory.

WHO-GMP Accreditation

Attained formal WHO-GMP compliance certification, expanding contract manufacturing operations to leading regional pharmaceutical distributors.

Injectable & Liquid Line

Commissioned sterile dry-powder injectable and automated liquid syrup filling facilities with Grade A laminar airflow cleanroom suites.

Pan-India Distribution

Expanded dedicated logistics and PCD franchise networks across 18+ states, distributing over 250 therapeutic formulations nationwide.
