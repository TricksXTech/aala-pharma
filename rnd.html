Scientific Innovation & Formulation R&D

Translating cutting-edge pharmaceutical sciences into stable, patient-friendly, and cost-effective therapeutic solutions.

Formulation Science

Bridging Molecular Science & Clinical Accessibility

At AALA Pharma, our dedicated Formulation Research & Development (FR&D) facility in Bareilly focuses on developing complex generics, novel drug delivery mechanisms, and fixed-dose combinations with superior stability and bioequivalence.

Our research team of formulation scientists, analytical chemists, and regulatory specialists works closely to deliver products that overcome physicochemical challenges such as poor solubility, hygroscopicity, and pediatric palatability.

  • Sustained Release (SR) and Controlled Release (CR) matrix technologies
  • Advanced taste-masking technologies for pediatric liquid orals
  • Complete Common Technical Document (CTD) dossier preparation
Research Pillars

Core Areas of Scientific Investigation

Our multi-disciplinary research framework spanning molecule characterization to clinical dossier filing.

Formulation Development

Pre-formulation screening, excipient compatibility studies, solid dispersion technologies, and prototype development across oral solids, liquids, and topicals.

Analytical Method Validation

Developing robust HPLC/GC/UV chromatographic methods, forced degradation studies, stress testing, and impurity identification following ICH Q2 guidelines.

Packaging Innovation

Engineering barrier packaging (tropical blister, cold-form Alu-Alu, amber USP Type III glass) ensuring formulation stability in hot and humid climates (Zone IVb).

Therapeutic Pipeline

Future Focus & Pipeline Formulations

Advancing therapeutics tailored to treat the evolving burden of lifestyle and infectious diseases in emerging markets.

Cardiovascular Combinations

Single-pill combinations of ARBs, CCBs, and statins designed to enhance patient adherence in chronic hypertension and hyperlipidemia.

Next-Gen Respiratory Inhalables

Dry powder inhaler (DPI) blends and metered-dose solutions providing targeted alveolar deposition with minimal systemic side effects.

Combatting AMR (Antimicrobial Resistance)

Beta-lactamase inhibitor synergistic combinations optimized for resistant hospital-acquired and community-acquired bacterial strains.

Pediatric & Geriatric Solutions

Dispersible tablets, oral dissolvable films, and pleasantly flavored suspensions catering to patients with swallowing difficulties (dysphagia).

Regulatory Affairs

Comprehensive Dossier & Technical Licensing Support

We provide ready dossiers in CTD (Common Technical Document) and ACTD formats including Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (COPP), stability reports, and analytical validation data.
